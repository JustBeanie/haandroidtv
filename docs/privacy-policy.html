HA Quick Access Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

HA Quick Access is an independent Android TV client for a Home Assistant server chosen and operated by you. It is not affiliated with Home Assistant.

Data handling

HA Quick Access does not collect, sell, share, or transmit your data to the app developer or to advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.

To work, the app stores on your device the Home Assistant server address, your tile and shortcut settings, and the last selected tile. Your long-lived Home Assistant access token is encrypted using an Android Keystore key that cannot be exported. Android backup and device-transfer backup are disabled for the app.

When you connect, the app sends your access token and control requests only to the HTTPS/WSS Home Assistant server address that you enter. That server is operated under its own privacy practices and is outside the developer's control.

Deletion

You can remove all locally stored connection information in the app by choosing the option to remove the connection. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data and the associated Android Keystore key.

Children

HA Quick Access is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app changes. For privacy questions, open an issue at the HA Quick Access issue tracker.
